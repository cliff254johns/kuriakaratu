How the Generator Works

Four ways to draft

Smart — pick a case type (ELC, Criminal, Succession, etc.); the generator pre-selects the right document templates.

Wizard — answer a few questions ("Is this a land matter?", "Is the client the plaintiff or defendant?"); the generator assembles the correct document.

Rule — pick documents directly; the generator combines pre-written clauses based on your choices (statutory period, interest, spousal consent, etc.).

AI — describe what you need in plain English; the generator drafts the body text. Requires an AI hook to be configured.

Custom templates

Any template you build yourself is stored locally and appears in the picker. Use {{placeholders}} to insert client, case, land, or demand fields — for example {{client.name}}, {{case.number}}, {{parcel.title}}.

Output

Generated documents use standard Kenyan court formatting (Times New Roman 12pt, double-ruled letterhead). You can print to PDF, download as .docx, download as .html, or save directly to the case file in the admin app.
