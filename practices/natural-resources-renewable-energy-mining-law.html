Preparing for an initial discussion

  • Prepare a short, non-confidential summary of the issue.
  • Note important dates and any known deadlines.
  • List relevant documents for discussion, but do not send confidential documents through this public website.
  • Identify the parties involved so the firm can consider potential conflicts.
