Kuria Karatu and Company Advocates is a litigation focused law firm committed to providing strategic, practical and results oriented legal solutions to individuals, businesses and institutions. We offer professional legal services.

Our approach is based on integrity, diligence, accessibility and attention to detail. We believe that every client deserves to understand their legal position, receive candid professional advice and have their interests pursued with diligence and commitment.

Whether you are facing a legal dispute, protecting your property, seeking compensation, navigating a transaction or simply need a sound legal advice, we are here to help you understand the law and protect what matters to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to some of the most common questions we receive.

What industries does Kuria Karatu And Company Advocates specialize in?
We have deep expertise in financial services, technology, real estate, energy, manufacturing, and the public sector, offering tailored legal solutions for each industry's unique regulatory environment.
Do you handle cross-border transactions?
Yes, we have a strong network of correspondent law firms across the EAC, Europe, Asia, and North America. We routinely advise on multi-jurisdictional transactions and disputes.
How can I schedule a consultation with a lawyer in Meru?
You can reach us via the contact form on our website, call our office directly at +254 725 799 752, or send a message on our WhatsApp button. We offer both in-person and virtual consultations.
What are your office hours?
Our offices are open Monday through Friday, from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are also available by appointment on weekends for urgent matters.
