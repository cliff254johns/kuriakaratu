Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

Kuria Karatu & Company Advocates aims to make its website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies or alternative ways of navigating the web.

1. Our approach

We aim to use clear page structure, meaningful headings, descriptive links, keyboard-operable controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts and text alternatives for meaningful images. We are working toward recognised accessibility practices, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, where reasonably applicable.

2. Current status

This statement is an accessibility commitment, not a claim that every page or third-party component has been independently audited or fully conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA. Some older content, documents, embedded media or third-party services may not yet be fully accessible.

3. Feedback and assistance

If you encounter a barrier, need information in an alternative format, or require assistance accessing a page, email info@kuriakaratuadvocates.com or call +254 725 799 752. Please tell us the page or service involved and the format or assistance you need. Do not include confidential case details in an accessibility request.

4. Review

We intend to review this statement as the Website changes and as accessibility issues are identified. A formal accessibility audit and remediation schedule should be arranged by the Website owner.
