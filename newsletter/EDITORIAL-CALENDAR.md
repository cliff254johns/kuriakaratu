# Kuria Karatu Legal & Business Review — Editorial Calendar

## Publication standard
- Publish one issue each calendar month.
- Each issue must contain at least six substantive editorial pages, with a distinct issue date, title, editor's review and source links.
- Plan each issue around varied subjects rather than repeating one practice area.
- Include practical takeaways, primary-source links and a clear statement that the publication is general information, not legal advice.
- Check the current text of legislation, regulations and judgments before publication. Flag proposals, bills and draft guidance as such.
- Do not publish client-identifying facts, confidential information or unsupported claims.
- Provide a print-friendly A4 version and an accessible web version.
- The public archive is at `/newsletter/`; each issue should have its own canonical URL and be added to `sitemap.xml`.

## Six-page issue structure
1. Lead feature: a significant legal or regulatory development, checked against primary sources.
2. Business and technology: practical risk, privacy, data, contracts or governance.
3. Property and infrastructure: transaction, development, land-use or construction topic.
4. People and work: employment, family, consumer or community issue.
5. Disputes and public affairs: litigation, ADR, procurement, public law or access to justice.
6. Practical checklist and closing perspective: actionable review points and sources.

## Publication checklist
- [ ] Confirm issue month, edition number and publication date.
- [ ] Review all legal statements against primary sources.
- [ ] Confirm every link works and is authoritative.
- [ ] Check each article is original, balanced and within the firm's actual practice scope.
- [ ] Remove confidential, identifying or privileged details.
- [ ] Obtain the firm's editorial approval before publishing.
- [ ] Check the six-page minimum in print preview.
- [ ] Add metadata, structured data, canonical URL and sitemap entry.
- [ ] Publish to the archive and link from the homepage and blog.
- [ ] Send only to people who have requested the newsletter; honour unsubscribe requests.

## Subscription note
The current public subscription action opens a prepared email to the firm's published contact address. It does not automatically store subscribers or send campaigns. Before bulk distribution, the firm should select and configure an approved mailing-list service, document the lawful basis and consent/notice, restrict access, set retention rules and implement unsubscribe handling.
