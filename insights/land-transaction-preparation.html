Land transaction preparation in Kenya

A careful land transaction begins with identifying the property, the parties, the nature of the interest being transferred and the records that need to be checked.

Key considerations

  • Identify the exact parcel and parties; check names and parcel descriptions across documents.
  • Arrange official searches and verification through the relevant land registry and authorised channels.
  • Review title history, restrictions, cautions, charges, easements, leases, consents and other entries.
  • Use a properly prepared written agreement and confirm completion steps, payments, consents, taxes and registration requirements with qualified advisers.
  • Keep records of signed documents, payments, consents and registration evidence.

Primary legal reference

Land Registration Act and related legislation. See Kenya Law – Laws of Kenya.
