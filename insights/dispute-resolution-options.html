Choosing between negotiation, mediation and arbitration

Different dispute-resolution processes suit different disputes. The right option depends on the contract, urgency, relationship, desired outcome, cost and whether a binding determination is needed.

Key considerations

  • Negotiation lets parties explore settlement directly or through representatives.
  • Mediation uses a neutral facilitator to help parties explore settlement; the mediator does not ordinarily impose a decision.
  • Arbitration is a private process in which an appointed arbitrator determines the dispute under the applicable agreement and law.
  • Court proceedings may be appropriate where urgent court relief or public determination is required.
  • Review any dispute-resolution clause and procedural rules before choosing a route.

Primary legal reference

Arbitration Act and relevant court rules. See Kenya Law – Laws of Kenya.
