Employment records every organisation should review

Clear employment documentation helps employers and workers understand the terms of work and workplace processes.

Key considerations

  • Written employment terms and records of changes.
  • Policies on conduct, discipline, grievances, leave, health and safety and workplace complaints.
  • Accurate payroll, leave, attendance and statutory contribution records, handled with appropriate privacy safeguards.
  • A fair, documented process for performance concerns, disciplinary matters and termination decisions.
  • A schedule for reviewing policies when legislation and operations change.

Primary legal reference

Employment Act and applicable labour legislation. See Kenya Law – Laws of Kenya.
