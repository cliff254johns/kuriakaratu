A data protection checklist for businesses

Businesses that collect or use personal data should understand what information they hold, why they use it, who can access it and how people can exercise their rights.

Key considerations

  • Map personal data, the people it relates to, purposes of use and storage systems.
  • Identify the organisation's role in each processing activity and document the lawful basis and transparency information required.
  • Limit access, establish retention periods and apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.
  • Review supplier and processor arrangements, cross-border transfers and incident-response procedures.
  • Create a route for data-subject requests and complaints; assess registration and other regulatory obligations.

Primary legal reference

Data Protection Act, 2019 and related regulations. See Kenya Law – Laws of Kenya and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
