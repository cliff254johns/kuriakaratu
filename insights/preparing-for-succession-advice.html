Preparing to seek succession advice

Organising information early can help a legal adviser understand family relationships, estate assets and immediate concerns.

Key considerations

  • Record the date and place of death and identify whether a will is known to exist.
  • Prepare a preliminary list of known assets, liabilities and institutions holding relevant records.
  • List immediate family members and other persons who may have a legal interest.
  • Note disputes, urgent preservation concerns, minors or dependants and steps already taken concerning estate property.
  • Obtain tailored advice before distributing or transferring estate assets.

Primary legal reference

Law of Succession Act and relevant probate rules. See Kenya Law – Laws of Kenya.
