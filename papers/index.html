For institutions, organisers & researchers

Publish a conference paper

Universities, professional associations, conference organisers, public institutions, NGOs and independent researchers may propose papers for inclusion. The publishing organisation is clearly credited on each paper record.

  • Submit the final, approved PDF and an editable source where available.
  • Provide the paper title, author names, affiliations, abstract and keywords.
  • Identify the publishing organisation, conference name, location and event dates.
  • Provide the publication date, DOI or permanent source link where available.
  • Confirm permission to host, distribute and share the PDF, including the applicable licence.
  • State whether the paper is peer-reviewed, conference-reviewed or an invited presentation.

Submissions are reviewed for completeness, attribution, permissions and suitability. Submission does not guarantee publication or peer-review certification.

Publication workflow

  1. Send the PDF and publication metadata.
  2. The editorial team checks attribution, file quality and distribution permission.
  3. The organisation approves the final public record.
  4. The paper is listed with its own shareable page, citation and PDF link.

Please attach the PDF to your email. Do not send confidential, unpublished or restricted material without the required permissions.
