Cookie Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

This policy explains cookies and similar technologies used on the Website and how you can control optional categories. A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags and scripts.

1. Our consent approach

Strictly necessary technologies may be used to make the Website function, protect it, remember a privacy choice, or provide a service you request. Optional analytics, personalisation and advertising technologies should not be activated unless you make the relevant choice. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the Website footer.

2. Cookie categories

  • Strictly necessary: security, network delivery, load balancing, and saving your cookie preferences. These cannot be switched off in the preference tool because the Website cannot reliably provide the requested functions without them.
  • Preferences: remember optional choices such as language or display settings, if those features are enabled.
  • Analytics: help understand aggregate Website usage and performance. These are optional and must remain disabled until consent is given.
  • Marketing: measure campaigns or support advertising and cross-site tracking. These are optional and must remain disabled until consent is given.

3. Current first-party consent cookie

The Website’s consent interface stores a first-party preference record named kk_cookie_consent for up to 180 days. It records the categories you choose and the time of the choice. It is used to remember your selection and is not an advertising cookie. The exact cookies set by the hosting provider, security/CDN services, embedded content or other third parties can vary with deployment configuration and must be checked against the live site.

4. Third-party services

Third-party content or services may set their own cookies when loaded. The Website should not load optional third-party scripts before consent. If a third-party service is introduced, this policy and the cookie inventory must be updated with its provider, purpose, cookie names, duration, and transfer information.

5. Manage your choices

Use “Accept all”, “Reject optional”, or “Customise” in the consent panel. You may reopen settings at any time from the footer. You can also delete cookies or block them through your browser settings; doing so may affect some Website functions. Browser controls do not necessarily withdraw consent recorded by a website, so use the Website preference panel as well.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the Website’s technology changes. For questions, contact info@kuriakaratuadvocates.com.

Deployment note: This policy describes the consent implementation added to the codebase. Before launch, the firm or its web administrator must inspect the deployed domain, hosting/CDN logs, embeds, and all loaded scripts to complete a verified cookie scan. Do not describe unverified cookies as absent.
