Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kuria Karatu & Company Advocates (“the firm”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you visit our Website, contact us, request information, or otherwise interact with us. It is intended to be read with our Data Protection Notice and Cookie Policy.

Confidentiality warning: A public website enquiry is not a secure channel for confidential legal instructions. Please do not submit privileged communications, identity documents, medical records, financial account details, or sensitive case evidence through an ordinary web form or social-media message.

1. Who is responsible?

The firm is the organisation responsible for deciding why and how personal data submitted directly to it through the Website and ordinary enquiries is used, subject to the role it performs in a particular legal engagement. Contact: info@kuriakaratuadvocates.com, +254 725 799 752, Meru, Kenya.

2. Information we may collect

  • Contact and identity details you choose to provide, such as name, email address, telephone number and organisation.
  • Enquiry details, including the practice area selected and a brief description of the assistance requested.
  • Communications and records of correspondence with the firm.
  • Technical information that may be generated when you use the Website, such as IP address, browser/device information, security logs, pages requested and timestamps, depending on hosting and security configuration.
  • Cookie and preference information as described in the Cookie Policy.

Do not provide more information than is necessary for an initial enquiry. If the firm accepts instructions, it will provide or agree any additional privacy information appropriate to the legal matter.

3. Purposes and lawful grounds

Depending on the context and applicable law, we may use information to respond to enquiries; assess whether the firm can act; perform a contract or take steps requested before entering one; comply with legal, regulatory, court, accounting and professional obligations; establish, exercise or defend legal claims; protect the Website and people from fraud or security threats; and manage consent and privacy requests. Where consent is the applicable ground, you may withdraw it without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

4. Professional confidentiality

Information received in the course of an accepted legal engagement may be subject to professional duties of confidentiality, legal privilege where applicable, court orders, and statutory obligations. This Website policy does not create privilege or guarantee that an unsolicited message is confidential. The firm will assess disclosures and access to matter information under applicable law and professional duties.

5. Sharing and recipients

We do not sell personal data. Information may be disclosed where necessary and lawful to authorised personnel; service providers supporting hosting, communications, security, document management or IT; professional advisers, insurers, auditors or regulators; courts, tribunals, law-enforcement bodies or public authorities where required or permitted; and other persons you authorise or whose involvement is necessary for the matter. Service providers should be subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

6. International transfers

Some service providers or infrastructure may process information outside Kenya. Where a transfer of personal data outside Kenya occurs, the firm must identify the destination, purpose and applicable safeguards and ensure the transfer meets the requirements of Kenyan data-protection law. Contact us for information about a specific transfer relevant to you.

7. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, taking account of enquiry handling, legal matters, limitation periods, professional obligations, court requirements, accounting, security and dispute resolution. Matter files may be retained for periods longer than general website enquiries. A specific retention schedule should be confirmed by the firm and applied to its systems.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use verified contact channels and avoid sending sensitive information through public forms.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful exemptions, you may have rights to be informed, access your personal data, request correction or deletion, object to processing, request restriction, withdraw consent, and exercise data portability where applicable. See Data Protection and Your Rights for how to make a request.

10. Children

The Website is not intended to invite children to submit personal data. Where a legal matter involves a child, information will be handled in accordance with applicable law, the child’s best interests and appropriate parental/guardian authority where required.

11. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. For privacy enquiries or requests, email info@kuriakaratuadvocates.com or call +254 725 799 752. Please mark the subject “Data Protection Request”.
