Client Care & Complaints Procedure

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

We aim to provide professional, courteous and clear legal services. If you are a current or former client and are concerned about our service, please tell us so that the issue can be reviewed.

1. Raise the concern with the firm

Contact the person handling your matter or write to info@kuriakaratuadvocates.com with the subject “Client complaint”. You may also call +254 725 799 752. Include your name, a safe way to contact you, the matter reference (if known), what happened, relevant dates and the outcome you are seeking. Do not send original documents or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Acknowledgement and review

The firm should acknowledge receipt, identify an appropriate person to review the concern, consider the file and relevant correspondence, and provide a reasoned response within a reasonable period. If more time is needed, the firm should explain why and provide an expected response date. A complaint should be handled impartially and with due regard to confidentiality, conflicts and professional obligations.

3. Urgent court deadlines

A complaint does not suspend court dates, statutory deadlines, limitation periods, payment obligations or other steps in a legal matter. If a deadline is approaching, contact the responsible advocate immediately and seek independent advice if necessary.

4. External professional complaint

If your concern relates to possible professional misconduct by an advocate, firm or employee, you may contact the Advocates Complaints Commission (ACC), which receives and investigates complaints concerning professional conduct. Its published contact details include acc@ag.go.ke and +254-20-2224029 / 251915. See the State Law Office explanation of the ACC process. The Law Society of Kenya also publishes information about professional complaints.

5. Data protection complaints

For concerns specifically about personal-data handling, first contact us through the Data Protection Notice. You may also use the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner complaints process.

Important: This page describes a proposed client-care route for the firm’s publication. The firm should approve and operationalise an internal complaint owner, escalation route, response targets and recordkeeping process before treating this as its formal service standard.
