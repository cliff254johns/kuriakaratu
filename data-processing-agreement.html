1. Scope and roles

This Agreement applies only to processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the identified services. The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing and warrants that its instructions and disclosures are lawful. The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions, including instructions concerning transfers, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

3. Confidentiality and security

The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process personal data are bound by confidentiality; implement appropriate technical and organisational measures proportionate to risk; restrict access to authorised personnel; and maintain procedures for secure handling, storage, transmission, backup and disposal. The parties shall document the specific controls in Schedule 2 rather than treating this general clause as proof of a particular certification.

4. Sub-processors

The Processor shall not appoint a sub-processor for the covered processing without the Controller’s prior written authorisation [specific or general authorisation — select and complete]. Where authorised, the Processor shall impose written data-protection obligations on the sub-processor and remain responsible for its performance to the extent required by law. Schedule 3 must identify approved sub-processors, services, locations and transfer safeguards.

5. Data subject requests and assistance

Taking account of the nature of processing, the Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in responding to data-subject requests and meeting applicable obligations, including security, breach notifications, impact assessments and regulatory consultations, to the extent relevant to the Processor’s role.

6. Personal data incidents

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting the covered data and provide information reasonably available to support assessment, mitigation and legally required notifications. The parties shall agree operational contacts and escalation timelines in Schedule 4. The Controller remains responsible for its statutory notifications unless the law provides otherwise.

7. Audit and compliance information

The Processor shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and permit proportionate audits or inspections on reasonable notice, subject to confidentiality, legal privilege, security and the rights of other clients. Audit scope, frequency, costs and safeguards should be specified in the underlying engagement or Schedule 5.

8. International transfers

No transfer outside Kenya may be made under this Agreement unless the parties have identified the destination, recipient, purpose, legal basis and safeguards and ensured compliance with applicable Kenyan law. Any additional jurisdiction-specific transfer terms must be attached where required.

9. Return and deletion

At the end of the services, the Processor shall, at the Controller’s choice, return or securely delete covered personal data, unless retention is required by law, professional obligations, legal claims, court directions or a documented lawful basis. Any retained data remains protected and must not be used for unrelated purposes.

10. Liability, term and precedence

Liability, indemnities, governing law, term, termination and precedence shall be set out consistently with the underlying engagement and applicable law. Nothing in this template excludes statutory rights or obligations.
